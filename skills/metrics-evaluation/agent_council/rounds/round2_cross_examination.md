# Round 2: Cross-Examination

Each reviewer received the other three reviews and had to agree, partially agree, or
disagree with their findings.

## Convergence

All roles agreed on:

- A universal validity route before any domain overlay.
- Explicit paired rules against zero and a predeclared useful margin.
- Correction of categorical AUC wording.
- Conditional economics and reporting.
- Stronger structured-output and representation contracts.
- Portable skill-level handoffs instead of paths into another skill's internals.

## Qualified Agreement

- The vision reviewer treated open-set semantics as part of a broader evaluator contract,
  not a standalone release issue.
- The statistical reviewer accepted boundary fixtures as blocking only when evaluator
  behavior was implemented or changed.
- The architecture reviewer opposed putting every specialist requirement in `SKILL.md`;
  requirements should remain in routed domain files.
- The representation reviewer accepted descriptive per-dataset synthesis when too few
  independent datasets support hierarchical inference.

## Withdrawn or Downgraded Proposals

- A displayed NDCG formula was downgraded from blocker to clarification.
- File length was treated as a usability concern rather than an automatic blocker.
- A full rewrite of representation guidance was rejected in favor of targeted contracts.
- Universal economics was rejected; only decision-facing claims require it.

## Round 2 Consensus Patch Set

The proposed set covered routing, paired decision rules, AUC wording, hypothesis-based
diagnosis, structured evaluator semantics, representation breadth and clustering,
multiplicity, conditional report profiles, and portable navigation.
